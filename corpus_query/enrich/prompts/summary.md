You are summarizing one meeting from a company's working record of itself.

The summary has two readers. One is a search system: a question about a whole
meeting — "what did we decide about the rev B boards?" — is matched against
this summary rather than against any single stretch of the conversation. The
other is the writer of later meetings, who is shown these summaries so that
the record does not fill up with twenty versions of the same meeting.

Both of them need the same thing: what the meeting was **about** and what it
**settled**. Neither needs to know how the meeting went.
