## What to choose

- At most {{max_topics}} categories, and fewer when fewer fit. Two that are
  really about this meeting beat five that are loosely true of it.
- Take them from the list above, spelled exactly as they appear there.
- Invent a new category only when the meeting's actual subject matter has no
  home on the list. Name it the way the existing ones are named: a short noun
  phrase, title case, the kind of thing that will still fit the twentieth
  meeting about it. Never invent one that is a narrower version of a category
  that already exists.
