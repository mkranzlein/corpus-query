# Seed Categories

The starting category list meetings are filed under. It is committed rather
than invented per run, so that the first meeting enriched is filed against the
same list as the last one, and so that two runs over the same corpus produce
comparable categories instead of two private vocabularies.

It is a starting point, not a closed set: enrichment adds a category when a
meeting's subject matter has no home here, and the dedupe pass folds
near-duplicates back together afterwards.
